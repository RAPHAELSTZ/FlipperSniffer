How to use

1. Open this page on your phone
2. Start scanning on your Flipper (GPS: Phone)
3. Type the session # shown on Flipper (e.g. a3f9b2)
4. Tap START, allow GPS access
5. Walk! Both devices record independently
6. When done, tap STOP and download the GPS track
7. On the dashboard, upload both files to merge
